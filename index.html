Mild Steel Aluminium
Steel replacement specialists

Royal Aluminium Rwanda Ltd is based in Kisimenti, Remera, and works across all of Kigali and the rest of the country. We handle two kinds of jobs: new aluminium door and window installations for houses, offices and commercial buildings under construction, and full replacements for old mild steel doors and windows that have started to rust, jam, or stop sliding and closing properly.

Every job starts with a site visit and a written quotation, priced in Rwandan Francs, valid for 7 days. No guesswork, no hidden costs added later.
